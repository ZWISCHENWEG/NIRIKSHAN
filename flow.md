# 🎙️ One important narration rule

Don't narrate what the viewer can already see.

Bad:

> “Now I am clicking the Argo float.”

Better:

> “Selecting an observation reveals the measured profile and gives us something we can verify against the model.”

Bad:

> “Now I click SAR mode.”

Better:

> “Once the underlying ocean state is understood, we can use that same current field to explore a response scenario.”

The narration should explain **why the action matters**, not what your mouse is doing.

|---|---|---:|
